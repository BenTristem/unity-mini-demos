Stop tunneling through walls with _physics_process

Two balls fall toward a wall. One uses _process, one uses _physics_process. Drop the FPS and watch one of them straight up phase through the floor like a ghost. That's a collision bug, not a feature.

_process — fps · tunneled: 0
_physics_process — Hz · tunneled: 0
_process FPS 60 FPS
Physics tick rate 60 Hz

What you're watching

  • _process(delta): runs every visual frame. delta varies. Use for rendering, animations, smooth follow-cameras.
  • _physics_process(delta): fixed timestep (60Hz by default). delta is constant. Use for physics, collisions, anything that needs determinism.
  • Move your velocity += GRAVITY * delta and move_and_slide() into _physics_process.
  • Low FPS won't break your physics. Low physics tick rate will.
Show me the GDScript 
extends CharacterBody2D

const GRAVITY := 980.0

# WRONG: runs every render frame, delta varies wildly
func _process(delta: float) -> void:
    velocity.y += GRAVITY * delta
    move_and_slide()  # can miss collisions at low FPS

# RIGHT: runs at fixed timestep, delta is constant
func _physics_process(delta: float) -> void:
    velocity.y += GRAVITY * delta
    move_and_slide()
