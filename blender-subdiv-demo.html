The Subdivision Surface modifier

Every smooth thing in Blender, every apple, every alien skull, every cute little mushroom, starts life as a blocky cube. One modifier, two sliders, and you're 80% of the way to a sculptable mesh. Drag and see.

Polys: 6 Verts: 8 Render: 6
Viewport level0
Render level0
Renderer will tessellate to 6 polygons at render time.

What's actually happening

  • Level 0 is your original mesh. Level 2-3 is enough for almost everything organic, characters, props, fruit, rocks.
  • Levels above 4 mostly burn polys you can't see. Save them for hero closeups and macro shots.
  • Viewport level is what you sculpt and edit against. Render level is what the renderer sees. Keep viewport low for performance, render high for the final image.
  • Catmull-Clark rounds corners smoothly, the default and what you want 95% of the time. Simple subdivides without smoothing, useful for adding detail (more verts to push around) without changing the silhouette.
Show me the Blender setup 
import bpy

obj = bpy.context.active_object

# Add the modifier
mod = obj.modifiers.new(name="Subsurf", type='SUBSURF')
mod.levels         = 2          # viewport
mod.render_levels  = 4          # render
mod.subdivision_type = 'CATMULL_CLARK'   # or 'SIMPLE'

# Or, in the UI: Modifier Properties → Add Modifier → Subdivision Surface
# Shortcut: Ctrl+1, Ctrl+2, Ctrl+3 to set level 1/2/3 on selected object.
