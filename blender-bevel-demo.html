The Bevel Modifier

Perfectly sharp edges scream "CG render." Real objects, even the crispest machined ones, catch a sliver of light right on the corner. Bevel is how you get that for almost no cost.

Why bevel everything

  • Real objects don't have perfectly sharp edges. A microscopic bevel is what makes a cube look like a thing instead of a render.
  • Width = how far the bevel extends from the edge. Start at 0.02–0.05 for most game props.
  • Segments: 1 (chamfer) is fine for low-poly. 3–4 reads as a "machined" edge. Above 6 is usually invisible at game distances.
  • Bevel modifier (non-destructive, live-tweakable) beats Edge Bevel (destructive) for almost every workflow.
Show me the Blender setup 
import bpy

obj = bpy.context.active_object

mod = obj.modifiers.new(name="Bevel", type='BEVEL')
mod.width        = 0.05
mod.segments     = 4
mod.profile      = 0.5
mod.limit_method = 'ANGLE'      # bevel only edges sharper than the angle
mod.angle_limit  = 0.523599     # ~30 degrees

# Shortcut: select the object, press Ctrl+B (in edit mode) for direct bevel,
# or add the Bevel modifier for non-destructive tweaking.
