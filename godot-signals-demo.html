Decouple everything with Signals

Your enemy dies and somehow needs to tell the UI, the audio system, and the score tracker. Wire them all up by hand? Nope. Godot has a built-in pager system for nodes, and once you get it, you'll never go back.

Why this matters

  • The emitter doesn't know who's listening. That's the whole point.
  • Connect or disconnect at runtime. Nothing breaks.
  • This is why Godot bakes signals into the language instead of leaving you to wire your own observer pattern.
Show me the GDScript 
# Define a signal on the emitter
signal hit(damage: int)

# Fire it
hit.emit(10)

# Connect a listener (Godot 4 syntax)
$Button.pressed.connect(_on_pressed)
hit.connect(_on_hit)

func _on_hit(damage: int) -> void:
    health -= damage
